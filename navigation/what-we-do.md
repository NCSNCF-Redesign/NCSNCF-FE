<section class="ncsncf__hero">
  <span class="ncsncf__badge">Our Programs & Initiatives</span>
  <h1>Empowering Lives Through Community, Housing, and Education.</h1>
  <p class="ocs__lead">
    Since our inception in 2011, the North County Special Needs Community Foundation has actively partnered with local civic leaders, educators, families, and developers to eliminate barriers and create accessible opportunities.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="ncsncf__section" style="margin-top: 3.5rem;">
  <h2>Key Program Areas</h2>
  
  <div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-3">
    <div class="ocs__card">
      <span class="ncsncf__badge">Housing</span>
      <h3>Independent Living Housing</h3>
      <p>Affordable, accessible housing for adults with developmental disabilities is critically limited. We spearheaded the 53-unit development opened in 2021 and the 60-unit Monte Vista project (opening Fall 2025 in Poway).</p>
      <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}" class="ocs__btn small">Housing Inquiries &rarr;</a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__card">
      <span class="ncsncf__badge">Sports & Recreation</span>
      <h3>Poway Padres & Athletics</h3>
      <p>For over 25 years, our Special Olympics softball team and sports programs have brought athletes and families together to learn teamwork, sportsmanship, and pride.</p>
      <a href="{{ '/calendar/' | relative_url }}" class="ocs__btn small">See Schedule &rarr;</a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__card">
      <span class="ncsncf__badge">Education & Jobs</span>
      <h3>Post-Secondary Training</h3>
      <p>Continuing education and meaningful workplace opportunities after high school help young adults gain valuable vocational skills, self-reliance, and financial independence.</p>
      <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}" class="ocs__btn small">Partner With Us &rarr;</a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__card">
      <span class="ncsncf__badge">Caregiver Support</span>
      <h3>2-Night Family Getaway Retreat</h3>
      <p>Recognizing the tremendous daily devotion of caregivers, the Foundation sponsors weekend retreats for qualified families to relax, recharge, and reconnect.</p>
      <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}" class="ocs__btn small">Inquire About Retreats &rarr;</a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__card">
      <span class="ncsncf__badge">Community Inclusion</span>
      <h3>Civic & Social Gatherings</h3>
      <p>From participation in the Poway Patriotic Parade to Special Presentations at City Hall and university athletics partnerships, we bring positive visibility to our community.</p>
      <a href="{{ '/about/' | relative_url }}" class="ocs__btn small">Our Impact &rarr;</a>
    </div>

    <div class="ocs__card">
      <span class="ncsncf__badge">Media & Advocacy</span>
      <h3>Documentary Film - "Playing with Heart"</h3>
      <p>Our documentary highlights the remarkable journeys of our athletes and families, educating the broader public about inclusion, resilience, and compassion.</p>
      <a href="https://www.ncspecialneedsfoundation.com/copy-of-media" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="ocs__btn small">Watch Trailer &rarr;</a>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
