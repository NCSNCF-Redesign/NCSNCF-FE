<section class="ncsncf__section" style="margin-top: 3.5rem;">
  <h2>Executive Leadership & Founders</h2>

  <div class="ncsncf__people">
    <article class="ncsncf__person">
      <h3>Tony Rubio</h3>
      <div class="ncsncf__role">Founder & President</div>
      <p>
        In 1998, Tony stepped up to coach the fledgling Poway Padres Special Olympics team alongside Nancy and Doug Greenwold. Over the past 25 years, Tony’s vision led to the founding of NCSNCF in 2011, establishing Poway’s premier independent living communities and fostering lifelong relationships among families.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article class="ncsncf__person">
      <h3>John Lingham</h3>
      <div class="ncsncf__role">Board Member & Community Advocate</div>
      <p>
        An SDSU graduate and renowned Poway restaurateur (Original Pancake House), John was recognized by the Entrepreneur’s Organization as Social Entrepreneur of the Year. He has served as Kiwanis Board Member, Senior Advisor to the Aktion Club, and St. Michael's Parish Finance Committee member, bringing extensive philanthropic leadership to NCSNCF.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article class="ncsncf__person">
      <h3>John Couvrette</h3>
      <div class="ncsncf__role">Board Member & CFO</div>
      <p>
        A USD alumnus in Accounting & Marketing, John brings deep executive and real estate expertise. Currently CFO of the Poway Chamber of Commerce and President of the Poway Kiwanis Club, John chaired the Chamber's Non-Profit Consortium and co-organized Jack-O-Smash, Poway's hallmark fundraising event.
      </p>
    </article>

    <article class="ncsncf__person">
      <h3>Nancy & Doug Greenwold</h3>
      <div class="ncsncf__role">Founding Visionaries & Coaches</div>
      <p>
        Original founders of the Poway Padres baseball program in 1998 with their son David, Nancy and Doug pioneered the vision of creating educational, social, and residential solutions for adults with special needs in the North County region.
      </p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
