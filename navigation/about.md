<!-- Our Story / Timeline -->
<div class="ncsncf__band">
  <div class="ncsncf__wrap">
    <h2>Our Story & Historical Milestones</h2>
    <p style="font-size: 1.1rem; line-height: 1.65; color: var(--text-muted); max-width: 48rem;">
      What started in 1998 as a single volunteer softball practice in Poway has grown into a comprehensive community foundation delivering housing, family respite, and community advocacy.
    </p>

    <ol class="ncsncf__timeline">
      <li>
        <strong>1998 &mdash; A Baseball Team Sparks a Movement</strong>
        <p>Nancy and Doug Greenwold founded the Poway Padres Special Olympics softball program. Tony Rubio joined as head coach, beginning a 25-year journey of athletic camaraderie and family support.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>2011 &mdash; Foundation Formally Established</strong>
        <p>NCSNCF was officially incorporated as a 501(c)(3) to address the urgent need for adult educational programs and independent living opportunities in Poway, California.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>2014 &mdash; Housing Coalition Formed</strong>
        <p>Collaborating with parents, city council members, and civic developers, the Foundation initiated plans to bring specialized residential housing to the community.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>2021 &mdash; Opening of 53-Unit Residential Complex</strong>
        <p>In June 2021, our coalition realized a major milestone: a 53-unit independent living complex opened its doors to adults with developmental disabilities.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Fall 2021 &mdash; Monte Vista Housing Approved</strong>
        <p>Meetings with Poway City Council led to the approval of a 60-unit affordable housing site across from Pomerado Hospital, allocating 30 units for Individuals with Developmental Disabilities and 30 for Veterans.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong>Fall 2025 & Today &mdash; Monte Vista Completion & Future Horizons</strong>
        <p>With Monte Vista scheduled to welcome residents, the Foundation continues expanding vocational education, family retreats, and recreational events.</p>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</div>
